Install

Open “Copy full signature”. Press Cmd+A, then Cmd+C. In Outlook for Mac: Outlook → Settings → Signatures → +. Name it “Stromy — Full”, paste normally with Cmd+V, and choose it as the account default for new messages. Repeat with “Copy reply signature”, name it “Stromy — Reply”, and choose it for replies/forwards.

For Outlook on the web: Open Outlook → Settings → Accounts → Signatures. Add the two signatures and choose their defaults.

Install a draft only after its missing details are confirmed. Verify the From address, all links and logo in a test email. Check Mac, web and phone separately; do not assume they sync.
